# Contact Us
CONTACT US
If you are interested in using Terrastories at our online server at <https://our.terrastories.app>, or discuss technology or partnership opportunities, please write to [support@awana.digital](mailto:support@awana.digital) and copy [terrastoriesorg@gmail.com](mailto:terrastoriesorg@gmail.com).
If you are looking for technical support for self-hosting Terrastories online or offline, please see channels to reach out on the [Support page](https://docs.terrastories.app/miscellaneous/support) on our documentation hub.
